|Tommy Douglas was a great Canadian because he led the government in Saskatchewan for 16 years, helped establish the New Democratic Party, and was also the “father” of Medicare.<br><br>  <br><br>A constitutional monarchy is a government system that finds a balance between powers and the people. |Constitutional Monarchies usually separate the government into the judiciary, legislature, and executive branches; even these branches are subject to further division. This divide can be seen in constitutional monarchies like Canada and the United Kingdom. This restricts a single entity from having overwhelming influence over the people.|   |
|The constitution in a constitutional monarchy gives rights and freedoms back to the people. These rights can include the right to vote and participate in government, while freedoms include the freedom of religion and association. The existence of a constitution limit the actions of power holders and oblige them to not overstep these rights and freedoms.|   |
|Through the constitution, many constitutional monarchies give citizens the opportunity to hold positions of power or vote out members of government if they are unhappy with the performance of the government. This puts more power into the hands of the people because the government has to be careful not to work against the will of the people.|   |
