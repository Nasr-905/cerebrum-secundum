### Task 1: Research Stage

#### 1.1 What civic issue interests you? What issue needs to be tackled to make your communities or the world better?

I am most interested by the current housing crisis that Toronto has been experiencing for the past few years. It is now significantly affecting the security and wellbeing of so many people.

  

#### 1.2 Who are the people and/or organisations involved in this issue? What views do they have on it?

Habitat for Humanity is a large organisation that is especially involved in shelter security for people, not just in Toronto but in the whole world. Their predominant view is that many homeless individuals and families just need a ‘leg-up’ in society to be able to provide for themselves. In following this belief the organisations aims to provide homes for families that have the potential to be successful with the advent of home ownership.

  

#### 1.3 What other perspectives might there be on this issue that are not commonly heard?

There exists a rising issue of gentrification as well. With rising housing prices, many who could once afford rent are forced to move into lower-cost housing or become homeless. At the same time, wealthier individuals replace them and eventually communities lose their heritage and culture, at the same time, economic inequality between districts will widen. Although this does not occur everywhere, it’s not a particularly rare occurrence.

  

#### 1.4 Which level(s) of government would be responsible for addressing this issue?

Housing prices are not rising in every part of Canada and cities experiencing high shelter insecurity do not necessarily have the same root cause. For this reason, interventions for addressing homelessness is a municipality’s responsibility.
