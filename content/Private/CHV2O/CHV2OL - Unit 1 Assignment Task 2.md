1. What will the name of your country be?  

The Democratic Federation of The Sabaody Archipelago

2. What is your basic belief about humanity?  

In Sabaody we build our government around the idea that people are innately good despite greed and selfishness existing as a smaller part of human nature as described by John Locke.

1. What form of government will you use?  

Sabaody, officially The Democratic Federation of The Sabaody Archipelago, uses a constitutional monarchy.

2. What process will you use to choose the government leader?  

The government leader of Sabaody is decided by a vote of all civilians. Every individual has an equal vote.

3. What will your leader's title be (e.g., Emperor, Leader, Boss, Monarch, President,   Grand Duke, Prime Minister, The Big Cheese, Pope, etc.)? 

The title of Sabaody’s government leader is President. A modest title that symbolizes their status as citizens of equal value.

4. What process will you use to make laws?  

We use a democratic system to approve laws in this country. Each citizen has a vote in the decisions, rules, and laws that are implemented and thus the will of the majority is reflected.

6. How will you maintain law and order in your country?  

Through the executive branch of the government we will employ a police department that will ensure people are following and respecting the laws. The department will be made up of citizens in order to further decentralize power. These citizens will undergo thorough training to make sure they represent the morals of the nation.
