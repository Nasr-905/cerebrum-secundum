The sun had barely set out on its journey across the sky, late after the wind began its own. My steps crackled the frosting grass and journeyed on. He was speaking to me, at least i think he was… Ramone was saying something about someone, I never listened to him much. But I suppose that’s why I liked him so much, because I never really had to listen… I never really had to be present. Words flow into the air from his throat and are frozen before they reach me. I’m isolated and he continued to speak. Once in a while i may say “uh huh” but not in response to what he may be saying, but as a reassurance that my words haven’t been frozen to their core. Before I regained awareness i noticed the grass could no longer be seen and the snow had covered the ground and suffocated all life below. My feet began to make a crunch rather than a crackle.
“But it doesn’t matter much, it was calm” These nearly frozen words made it to my ears.

“Yeah right” I knew better than anyone that I was as close as Ramon ever got to a friend.

The crunch of my boots deepened as the snow grew thicker. At some point along the way it began to reach my ankles. I started to realise that I was cold, very cold. The autumn jacket and scarf didn’t suffice in this weather. But alas, my destination came forth as salvation in my idle position. The small yellow and black vehicle with its limited number of seats and infinite amount of space. Though it lacked the heat I longed for, the warmth of the others will suffice.
“Get In! You’re late!” The operator bellowed as she directed for the doors to be opened.
The steps into salvation as we boarded the vehicle felt few but painfully slow. But we reached the summit and the faces of those we shared the mechanism with soured the taste of salvation. 
“Eww! What’s on your face Ramone?” one of the faces spoke. Though immature and ill considered, it at least had the ability to do that.

“Ramone! Kirta! Behave yourselves!” The operator’s voice left my ears ringing.
