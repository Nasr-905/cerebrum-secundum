A war has just begun
A war on terror
Says a wolf in sheep's fur

"A war against animals!"
Says a man in a small hat
He calls it a kippah
But the hat calls him a mad man

Bombs dropped
Mothers die
Sons die
Daughters die
But only on one side
Is this a war...
Or a genocide?

Soon the world may not care
About the child who lost their home
Their family
And food on their stove
But we must not forget
That the lightest coffins
Are the hardest to hold
