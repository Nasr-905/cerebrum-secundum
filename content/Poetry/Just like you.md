I sat by the windowsill

As the sun says hello

On a new day— a true day, Although...

The birds sing in red

And butterflies fly through blue

Who would've thought, I'd find a girl...

Just like you?


If your heart is a drug

Can you lace it with love?

And I'll trade it for a song that I've sung


I'll sing, your favorite song

Again and again

I would love, that you would love—

To sing along


We'll sing,

This world was not made for us

We'll fly away and find a purple chorus

And when they look for us

We'll be high off the angel dust-
