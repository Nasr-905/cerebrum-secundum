I woke up next to the bus station,

The fall is what was left for me

The air beneath my cardigan chills

The Calgary weather begins to get to me


No Others surround me

Not even my brother, who would hide behind the pine trees,

I— I was lost;

But The silence was calming,

The wind stood still,

I felt the world stopping

There’s nothing I couldn’t feel,

but— But the winter chill...

It rose from where it lay

And from downhill the bus came,

The yellow travels slow in the snow

Until I look through the window...

Who knows what is there for me,

Now the apprehension is warming me,

It— It was spring

The chill remained,

so In the shade I stay;

The Warmth wasn’t worth...

The Burn it brings...

And the Light it shines...

Is too Bright for me...

So I stay— I stay


The bus has now arrived,

but The windows are tinted black,

or maybe it’s just dark inside...

I Should get a view from the front—


Time is up— Death is here— Say goodbye
