Constitutional Monarchy: The Middleground

Many forms of government exist all around the world, from autocracies to republics, and with each exists some pros and cons. A certain form of government, the constitutional monarchy, finds the best balance in power distribution, election processes and civilian freedoms. The main selling point for this type of government system is how it is made to operate within the bounds of a constitution. This ensures that the rights and freedoms are not violated and that power holders are chosen justly every time. A democratic constitution ensures the basic rights to vote, run for office and a rule of law that judges everyone equally. Lastly a constitutional monarchy ensures that there exists a constituent face or symbol of the government which can induce a stronger sense of connection between the people and the state.
