I even tried my hand at developing addons that extend the anki experience further than what the app originally is capable of.

These experience taught me a lot about collaboration and how to work with others from anywhere at any time.

My most successful project was a deck for memorizing Quran called SAAT, which stands for seven words at a time. This, however, was not something I could have done on my own. Little did I know, but I would be sorting the Quran into over 10,000 segments each with their own audios and translations. It was obvious from early on that I needed a team. I gathered two other programmers and we got to work designing the cards procedurally. I worked on word-by-word translations, while the others worked on the audio and visual user experience. Although I didn't realize it at the time, I was a leader in this group and had the responsibility to ensure that everyone is successful. 

  

Q1.Tell me about your favourite teacher. What made this teacher so special?

My math teacher from grades 11 and 12. It was his first year teaching his own class. His teaching seemed a bit awkward and even inexperienced at times. But he had so much passion for the field of mathematics. He would spend his time making each class interactive engaging and worthwhile. He brought himself down to our level at points when he admitted he was unsure about something, he was open about being wrong in the previous lessons, he wasn't afraid to be slightly flawed. This teacher was the reason for my success in math in my final years. He brought motivation and innovation to a class that I felt had very little of that at all. My confidence in my mathematical ability has increased considerably since joining his class. For the next few years, I'll need to provide this passion on my own for all my courses, not to say I'll have to do it alone. I'll have friends and study groups to help spark that passion and keep it going throughout my undergraduate career at McMaster University.
