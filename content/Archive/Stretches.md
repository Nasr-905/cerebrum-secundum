**- Static stretches are best
    
- Stretches should be held for 30 seconds
    
- 10 min total stretching duration per week
    
- 3 sets of 30 seconds and 30 second rest for 6 days a week
    
- A gentle and controlled stretching with low intensity will produce greater flexibility
    
- After regular workout
